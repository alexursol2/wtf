1 Fund escrow

An order is only as good as the escrow behind it. The venue holds encrypted balances directly, so deposit before posting — an unfunded ask settles for zero rather than failing, because a revert would leak the shortfall. Deposit shares to post an ask, cash to fill one.

Wrap and unwrap amounts are public — they ride a plaintext ERC-20 transfer. Confidentiality applies to balances and transfers between wrapped holders, not to the custody boundary itself. That is true of every ERC-7984 wrapper and is stated in the leak model rather than glossed.

Known gap: venue escrow is not backed by this wrapper. The venue credits depositShares as a self-declared encrypted amount and never calls the wrapper, so escrow is unbacked. Settlement between parties is still exact; what is missing is the link tying escrow to real custody. Documented in the README, not papered over.

Holder register

disclosed by the issuer on request — not automatic

Connect a wallet to check register access.

Fill volumes are granted automatically; the register is not. It stays sealed until the issuer calls grantRegisterAccess for a named holder. Grants are permanent — Nox has no removeViewer and no persistent disallow. What a grant does not do is follow the holder: the next transfer mints a fresh handle the grantee cannot read. Rotation, not revocation.

Grants in Nox are permanent — there is no revocation primitive, and a published price or volume can never be un-published. Amount-gated compliance rules are not enforced on the encrypted path. Both are documented limitations, not oversights; see the README leak model.
